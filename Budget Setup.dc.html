Getting started

Set up your monthly expenses and shared expenses so you can tell from the start whether you are on or off track. Use the salary box to indicate your salary and the app will calculate how much money you have left as you add an expense.

  1. 1 Drop a CSV or Excel file with 2 columns — Bill name and Monthly Target. The system will try to organise it against a category. You can change the category if you wish.
  2. 2 If you share a joint account with a partner, tick the joint account box — it will calculate how much you should pay into it each month.
  3. 3 You can then edit the rows in the budget lines and hit Save.

Name your bills, set a monthly target, tag a category.

Bill names are yours alone — call a line whatever you call it. The category is the tie back to your bank statements, so pick one that matches how the transactions are labelled.
